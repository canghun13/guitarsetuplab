Guitar Repair Intake Workflow

Capture customer language, instrument identity, included items, permissions, and an initial condition baseline.

Keep three voices separate

The customer report describes what they experienced. The intake observation records visible facts. The diagnosis is created only after authorized inspection. Keeping these sections separate prevents assumptions from becoming shop history.

Close the loop

Tag included accessories, confirm communication and budget preferences, issue a job number, and connect approved work to a quote and job sheet. The intake record is a practical workflow document, not a liability waiver.

Safety boundary Stop for abnormal resistance, cracking sounds, visible movement, electrical hazards, or measurements you cannot reproduce.
