String Gauge Changes and Setup Effects

Plan gauge or tuning changes around published tension data and follow-up setup checks.

Tension is not gauge alone

Unit weight, vibrating length, and frequency determine tension. Two equal-diameter strings with different construction can have different unit weights.

Recheck the system

After a meaningful tension change, stabilize tuning and recheck relief, tremolo balance, nut movement, action, and intonation in that order. Do not cut a nut slot from a calculator result.

Compare supported tensions and then use the gauge-change planner.

Safety boundary Stop for abnormal resistance, cracking sounds, visible movement, electrical hazards, or measurements you cannot reproduce.
