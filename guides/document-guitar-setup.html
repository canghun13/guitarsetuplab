How to Document a Guitar Setup

Create a useful before/after record with repeatable measurement points and test conditions.

Record the baseline

Name the instrument, tuning, string set, climate, measurement fret, and playing position. A number without its method is difficult to repeat.

Separate observation from work

Write the customer symptom, technician observation, measurements, work performed, and final test as separate sections. Record one-variable changes and include unresolved limits rather than promising an outcome.

Open the measurement sheet or create a before/after card.

Safety boundary Stop for abnormal resistance, cracking sounds, visible movement, electrical hazards, or measurements you cannot reproduce.
