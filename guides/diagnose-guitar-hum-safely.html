How to Diagnose Guitar Hum Safely

Isolate guitar noise with substitution tests while staying outside amplifiers and mains equipment.

Work from outside inward

  1. Turn down monitoring level before changing connections.
  2. Test one known-good cable directly into a known-safe amplifier or interface.
  3. Compare another instrument, room, and power circuit.
  4. Observe pickup-position, control, and touch behavior.
  5. Document recent wiring or jack symptoms.

Do not open an amplifier, power supply, or mains device. Stop for shocks, heat, odor, damaged power cables, or intermittent mains behavior.

Run the Ground Hum Diagnostic.
