How to Measure Neck Relief

A repeatable capo, last-fret, and feeler-gauge method for recording relief.

Measurement method

  1. Tune the instrument and hold it in playing position.
  2. Capo the first fret.
  3. Hold the string at the last fret or neck/body joint.
  4. Measure the gap near the middle of the span with a feeler gauge.

Record where you measured and which string you used. A value alone does not prove that the truss rod should be adjusted.

Safety boundary Stop for abnormal resistance, cracking sounds, visible movement, electrical hazards, or measurements you cannot reproduce.
