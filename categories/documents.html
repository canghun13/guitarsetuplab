Evidence standard

Browser-generated records stay on the device unless you save or print them. Apply your own privacy, retention, tax, consent, and consumer-law procedures.

  1. Preserve the starting condition.
  2. Change or test one variable.
  3. Retune or reset the test condition.
  4. Repeat the same measurement.
  5. Record remaining uncertainty.

When to stop

Stop when evidence conflicts, a datum is unclear, hardware reaches a limit, or the next step becomes structural, energized, or irreversible. A qualified technician can inspect conditions that browser inputs cannot represent.
