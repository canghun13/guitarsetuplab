How to Document a Guitar Setup

Create a useful before/after record with repeatable measurement points and test conditions.

Record the baseline

Name the instrument, tuning, string set, climate, measurement fret, and playing position. A number without its method is difficult to repeat.

Separate observation from work

Write the customer symptom, technician observation, measurements, work performed, and final test as separate sections. Record one-variable changes and include unresolved limits rather than promising an outcome.

Open the measurement sheet or create a before/after card.

Safety boundary Stop for abnormal resistance, cracking sounds, visible movement, electrical hazards, or measurements you cannot reproduce.

Scope of this guide

Create a useful before/after record with repeatable measurement points and test conditions. Use How to Document a Guitar Setup to make a repeatable decision for this named task, not to impose one specification on every instrument.

Practical example

A reproducible entry names tuning, string set, relief span, action fret, pickup measurement condition, and the final full-neck test. Preserve an unresolved rattle or limited bridge travel in the after record instead of turning “improved” into an unsupported promise.

Record the conditions

For How to Document a Guitar Setup, write down instrument type, tuning, installed strings, relevant hardware, climate when material, physical datum, unit, tool resolution, and the result before and after change. A result from this guide is comparable only when its method travels with the number.

Verification checklist

  1. For How to Document a Guitar Setup, confirm the starting state and exact question.
  2. Repeat its critical measurements from the same reference.
  3. Compare its result with direct observation and applicable maker documentation.
  4. Change one reversible variable where the task permits.
  5. Retest the named symptom and preserve unresolved exceptions.

Related workflow

  • setup measurement sheet — continue when that page answers the next unresolved measurement or decision.
  • before after setup card — continue when that page answers the next unresolved measurement or decision.

Limits

How to Document a Guitar Setup cannot inspect hidden structure, material condition, service history, or player technique. Stop this guide workflow for electrical hazards, abnormal resistance, structural movement, conflicting readings, or unplanned material removal.
