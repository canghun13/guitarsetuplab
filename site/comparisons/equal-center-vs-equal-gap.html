Equal-Center vs Equal-Edge-Gap String Spacing

Compare the visual, tactile, and measurement consequences of two string-spacing methods.

MethodWhat stays equalResult with mixed gauges
Equal centerDistance between every adjacent centerlineVisible edge gaps become smaller beside heavier strings.
Equal edge gapOpen space between adjacent string edgesCenter distances grow where neighboring strings are heavier.

Neither method supplies a universal margin or ergonomic target. Nut width, outer-string position, fret bevel, playing style, and real gauges remain design inputs.
