Decision comparison

250k vs 500k vs 1M Guitar Pots

Choose a passive guitar pot range by pickup loading, brightness goal, cable context, and control layout.

Nominal valueTypical starting contextGeneral loading trend
250kΩMany passive single-coilsMore loading; often a warmer result
500kΩMany passive humbuckers and mixed designsLess loading; often more high-frequency response
1MΩSpecial brightness or vintage-specific designsVery light loading; can expose harshness/noise

Choose by the whole load

These are common starting ranges, not compatibility rules. Pickup inductance/resistance, cable capacitance, amplifier input, tone network, pot tolerance, and control wiring all matter. Active systems often use manufacturer-specified lower values.
