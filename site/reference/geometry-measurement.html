Luthier Geometry Measurement Definitions

Define chord, sagitta, spread, datum, projection, break angle, and calibration measurements.

Datum / witness point
The physical reference actually touched by the string, fret, straightedge, or measuring tool.
Chord and sagitta
Chord is the straight endpoint-to-endpoint width; sagitta is the perpendicular center depth to the arc.
Spread
An overall center-to-center distance unless a tool explicitly says edge-to-edge.
Projection distance
The horizontal run used with a measured rise to calculate an angle.
Break angle
The side-view angle between the string segment and the reference plane at a witness point.
Calibration
A comparison between a commanded printed length and its physical measured length.

Never mix inches and millimetres inside one entry list. Repeat measurements and preserve the raw values before rounding.
