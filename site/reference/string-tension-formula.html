Guitar String Tension Formula Reference

Understand unit weight, scale length, frequency, units, and the supported data boundary.

Published formula

T = UW × (2 × L × F)² ÷ 386.4, where T is pounds-force, UW is pounds per linear inch, L is scale length in inches, and F is frequency in hertz.

The matcher uses a limited subset of D’Addario XL published unit weights. It does not infer unit weight from diameter and does not claim that another brand or construction has the same tension.

Working limits

Scale is the vibrating length. Tuning frequency must match the intended octave. Results use ideal-string reference data; installation, construction tolerance, speaking length, and measurement conditions introduce differences.

Scope of this reference

Understand unit weight, scale length, frequency, units, and the supported data boundary. Use Guitar String Tension Formula Reference to make a repeatable decision for this named task, not to impose one specification on every instrument.

Practical example

At fixed unit weight and scale, dropping one whole step multiplies frequency by about 0.891 and tension by about 0.794. Exact comparisons between products still require their published unit weights; diameter alone cannot supply the missing construction data.
