How to use this tool

  1. Define the physical datum, units, and measurement method.
  2. Enter observations without guessing at measurements.
  3. Compare the result with a known fixture before marking material.

Method, assumptions & limits

This tool applies an explicit geometry model to the measurements shown in its result. Rounding is for display; calculations retain full precision. Calibration bars verify browser/PDF scale, but only a physical measurement verifies a printer.

Stop if… you feel abnormal resistance, hear cracking, see wood movement or structural deformation, find an unknown truss-rod condition, or cannot make the required measurement. Do not remove material until the cause is confirmed.
