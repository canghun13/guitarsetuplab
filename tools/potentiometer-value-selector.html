How to use this tool

  1. Bring the instrument to playing condition and tune it.
  2. Enter observations without guessing at measurements.
  3. Follow the low-risk checks in order and record changes.

Method, assumptions & limits

This tool organizes setup evidence; it cannot inspect wood, frets, hardware, or electrical safety. Reference values are comparison points, not universal targets. Playing style, instrument design, string choice, and climate matter.

Stop if… you feel abnormal resistance, hear cracking, see wood movement or structural deformation, find an unknown truss-rod condition, or cannot make the required measurement. Do not remove material until the cause is confirmed.
