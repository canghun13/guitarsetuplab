Scope of this comparison

Compare availability, relative output, clarity, and noise-canceling tradeoffs for four-conductor humbuckers. Use Series vs Parallel vs Coil Split to make a repeatable decision for this named task, not to impose one specification on every instrument.

Practical example

Series usually provides the fullest humbucker output; parallel is generally clearer and may retain cancellation; a split normally lowers output and loses full cancellation. Confirm accessible coil leads and switch contacts before choosing a mode.
